## 1. Candidate Mathematical Formulations

### 1.1 Outgoing Advancement Models ($E_{out}$)

Let $r_{atk} \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ be the attacker's advancement star count.
When military books are active, $E_{out} = 0.02 \times r_{atk} \in [0.00, 0.10]$.
Let $OI$ be ordinary outgoing increase, $OD$ be ordinary outgoing decrease, and $Crit$ be critical bonus.

- **ADV-OUT-A (Same-Pool Additive — Canonical Default)**:
  $$A_{out} = \max(-0.90, OI + E_{out} - OD)$$
  $$F_{out} = 1.0 + A_{out} + Crit$$

- **ADV-OUT-B (Independent Multiplier)**:
  $$A_{skill} = \max(-0.90, OI - OD)$$
  $$F_{out} = (1.0 + A_{skill} + Crit) \times (1.0 + E_{out})$$

- **ADV-OUT-C (Independent-Before-Clamp)**:
  $$F_{out} = \max(0.10, (1.0 + OI - OD + Crit) \times (1.0 + E_{out}))$$

- **ADV-OUT-D (Base-Layer Multiplier)**:
  $$B' = B_0 \times (1.0 + E_{out})$$

### 1.2 Incoming Advancement Models ($E_{in}$)

Let $r_{tgt} \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ be the target's advancement star count.
When military books are active, $E_{in} = 0.02 \times r_{tgt} \in [0.00, 0.10]$.
Let $II$ be incoming increase (vulnerability) and $ID$ be incoming damage reduction.

- **ADV-IN-A (Same-Pool Additive — Canonical Default)**:
  $$A_{in} = \max(-0.90, II - ID - E_{in})$$
  $$F_{in} = 1.0 + A_{in}$$

- **ADV-IN-B (Independent Multiplier)**:
  $$A_{skill} = \max(-0.90, II - ID)$$
  $$F_{in} = (1.0 + A_{skill}) \times (1.0 - E_{in})$$

- **ADV-IN-C (Independent-Before-Clamp)**:
  $$F_{in} = \max(0.10, (1.0 + II - ID) \times (1.0 - E_{in}))$$

## 2. Mathematical Proof of Observational Equivalence Under Discrete RNG

### 2.1 Theoretical Gap Analysis

Consider the difference in the final multiplier between Independent Multiplier (Model B) and Same-Pool Additive (Model A):

$$\Delta_{out} = F_{out}^{(B)} - F_{out}^{(A)} = (1 + A_{skill})(1 + E_{out}) - (1 + A_{skill} + E_{out}) = A_{skill} \times E_{out}$$

For standard combat encounters:
1. $E_{out} \le 0.10$ (maximum 5 red stars = $10\%$).
2. In typical engagements without extreme stacking, $|A_{skill}| \le 0.30$.
3. Thus, $|\Delta_{out}| \le 0.30 \times 0.10 = 0.030$ ($3.0\%$).

### 2.2 Discrete RNG Masking Effect

In the Stage2 Base Damage Formula, discrete random fluctuation $R \in \{86, 87, 88, 89, 90, 91, 92, 93, 94\}$ introduces a mandatory spread factor of:

$$\text{RNG Span} = \frac{94 - 86}{100} = 0.080 \quad (8.0\%)$$

For any base damage $B_0$:
- The legal prediction interval for Same-Pool is $[0.86 \times B_0 \times F_{out}^{(A)}, \; 0.94 \times B_0 \times F_{out}^{(A)}]$.
- The legal prediction interval for Independent is $[0.86 \times B_0 \times F_{out}^{(B)}, \; 0.94 \times B_0 \times F_{out}^{(B)}]$.

Because the model gap $|\Delta_{out}| \le 0.030$ is strictly smaller than the RNG spread ($0.080$), **the two prediction intervals overlap by at least $62.5\%$ to $100\%$ across all standard battle events**.

### 2.3 Mathematical Theorem: Observational Equivalence
> **Theorem**: Let an observed integer damage value $D_{obs}$ be generated by the underlying battle engine. Unless $|A_{skill}|$ is exceptionally large ($> 0.70$) AND base damage $B_0$ is sufficiently high ($> 1500$) such that the integer interval endpoints decouple into mutually disjoint non-overlapping sets, $D_{obs}$ is simultaneously admissible by BOTH Model A and Model B.
> 
> Therefore, within standard natural battle replays, Model A (Same-Pool) and Model B (Independent Multiplier) are **OBSERVATIONALLY EQUIVALENT**.
